    Reports

    Download a month's sales and store data as an Excel workbook.

    The workbook has these sheets:

    Summary
    Orders, sales, average order value, units sold, orders by status, new accounts
    Orders
    Every order with customer, delivery details, status and subtotal
    Order lines
    One row per product in each order, with quantities and prices
    Product sales
    Units, orders and sales per product, best sellers first
    Daily sales
    Orders, cancellations, units and sales for every day of the month
    Customers
    Who ordered, how often and how much
    New accounts
    Customers who registered on the website that month
    Catalogue
    Current products, prices and stock
    Sales figures leave out cancelled orders. The report contains customer contact details; store and share it carefully.
